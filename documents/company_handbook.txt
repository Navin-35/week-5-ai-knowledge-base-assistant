DStarix Employee Handbook

Company Overview

DStarix is an AI-focused technology company specializing in Generative AI, Machine Learning, Large Language Models (LLMs), Cloud Computing, and Enterprise AI Solutions. The company develops intelligent applications to help businesses automate workflows and improve productivity.

Working Hours

Employees work from Monday to Friday.

Office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

Lunch break is from 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM.

Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to:

20 Annual Paid Leaves

10 Sick Leaves

5 Casual Leaves

Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with prior manager approval.

Employees must never share passwords.

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all company accounts.

Only authorized personnel may access confidential company data.

• Hands-on AI projects

• Weekly learning sessions

• Code reviews

• Career guidance
